Cinnamoroll for Windows

Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit Intel/AMD.
Extract the entire ZIP, then double-click Cinnamoroll.exe.
Keep SDL3.dll beside the executable. No Rust, Python, or installer is needed.
The system-tray icon may be inside the ^ overflow area beside the clock.

Click the pet to interact. Drag to pick him up.
While the pet is focused:
Q: Little pocket  S: Settings   T: Chase / stop   E: Hide and peek from an edge
F: Feed       N: Nap / wake    H: Peek where I point
D: Dance      R: Read         P: Head pats
O: Offer treat Y: Ball        I: High five   U: Nuzzle
K: Tap dance rhythm  Space: Pause  Escape: Quit

Chasing: chase, pause, crouch, hold ready for two seconds, then pounce and miss.
After five seconds without getting close, he pants for five seconds and resumes.
Edge peeking: click the visible half or press E to come back out.

Music reactions use Windows media sessions. Browser music works when the
browser exposes a media session; video and podcasts can also trigger it.
Typing-cursor following is optional and supports apps that expose a caret.

This is an unsigned prototype. The 64-bit executable was cross-built on a Mac
and its DLL dependencies checked. Native Windows runtime testing remains
necessary. Check-Windows.ps1 is an optional five-second launch check; quit
any running copy before using it. Please also test real dragging, display
scaling, settings, and media reactions on the Windows laptop.

The control panel groups Companion, Study, Playroom, and Animations under
Together. Cozy corner, Appearance, Behavior, Connections, and App settings hold preferences.
Start/pause/skip/extend focus and break timers in Study. Set up a bed and
blanket in Cozy corner; choose a treat, ball, or bubbles in Playroom. Drag a treat near the pet; drag and
release a ball to throw it. Put toys away from Playroom. Study sessions
are quiet, pause for toys, and require Resume to continue.
Drag the blanket to move both when he is settled on it. Grab the pet
directly to lift him away from the blanket with his hanging animation.

Right-click the pet or press Q for Play, Cozy time, Study, Creative corner,
Dress up, and Seasonal moments. Drag props to play directly. Choose a
subject preset to start focus; keep one local task and mark it done.
Tea, blankets, plushes, pillows, cups, ribbons, a wand, paper planes,
gift parcels, accessories, seasonal poses, palette play and transparent
sticker export are available from the pocket. Stickers go to
Pictures/Cinnamoroll, or Documents if Pictures is unavailable.
Quiet surprises wait several minutes and respect focus, naps, reduced
motion and low battery. They can be disabled in Behavior.

App settings includes launch at login and signed GitHub updates. Automatic
checks and updates after one hour away start on. Study sessions defer
automatic installation. Downloads and startup registration are per user.
