# Cinnamoroll v0.3.0 · A little more awake ☁️

More little adventures between naps, with invitations you can answer right on your desktop.

## A day of his own

- **Nine rotating awake routines:** tea, reading, plush hugs, ear wiggles, dancing, cloud play, waving, stretching and curiosity. Each gets a turn before repeating; seasonal moments can still appear.
- **More visible moments:** the first quiet surprise is scheduled after roughly 1–2 minutes, then every 2–4 minutes when eligible. Longer quiet-time preferences take priority. Reading, tea, plush hugs and play linger a little longer.
- **A paw just for you:** with touch reactions enabled, an occasional wave becomes a raised-paw invitation. Hold your pointer near that paw for 0.8 seconds to complete the high five. It ends quietly after ten seconds if ignored.
- **Toy invitations:** surprise items arrive with a little wave and wait for you to use them. Balls join the selection alongside treats, brushes and other small props.
- **A cozy plush ritual:** near his corner, he can collect a plush, hug it, carry it home and settle awake with it beside him. Pick him or the plush up to take control.
- **Welcome back:** after a break, a greeting can lead into a small stretch.

These moments use the existing native renderer and animation strips, without new dependencies or settings sections. Focus sessions and deliberate naps remain undisturbed. Automatic moments respect their existing switches, reduced motion, low battery and direct interactions.

## Sleep and surprises

Fixed a post-chase recovery nap that could last indefinitely and block later activities. Recovery now ends after 45 seconds while the computer is active. Genuine computer-idle sleep still wakes when you return; deliberate naps remain tucked in until you wake him.

Surprise items default to **On** for new settings and older settings without that field. An explicitly saved **Off** remains Off. Find the switch at **Settings → Playroom → Surprise items → Manage**.

The existing **Sleep when idle** preference still applies. Reading or watching a lecture without keyboard or mouse input can count as computer idle; increase that timeout if you prefer him awake for longer.

## A tidier playroom

- Toys and affection controls have separate, aligned groups.
- Surprise-item status and its Manage button are easier to find.
- Tidying toys has a smaller secondary action.
- Touch reactions have their own compact preference row.
- Dream bubbles scale with the pet and sit close to his head, including at smaller sizes and near display edges.

## Install or update

Existing installations can use **App settings → Check for updates**, or their enabled automatic update preferences. The one-line installers install the latest stable release. Saved preferences remain local.

Downloads:

- Apple Silicon Mac: `Cinnamoroll-0.3.0-aarch64-apple-darwin.zip`
- Intel Mac: `Cinnamoroll-0.3.0-x86_64-apple-darwin.zip`
- Windows x64: `Cinnamoroll-0.3.0-x86_64-pc-windows.zip`
- Source: `Cinnamoroll-0.3.0-source.zip`

macOS 13+ and Windows 10/11 x64 remain supported. Signed update archives use the existing trusted update key. The Mac app is ad-hoc signed and not notarized; the Windows executable is unsigned. Update archive signatures are separate from OS distribution signing.
