# Cinnamoroll v0.2.0 · Little moments, together ☁️

Eleven new ways to play, study, and make a little room for your work.

## Small wonders

Open **Little moments** in his right-click pocket:

- **Gentle grooming:** stroke his head or ears with a soft brush. A new eight-frame grooming strip plays without restarting on every stroke, with accessory anchors for each pose.
- **Dandelion puff:** offer it near his nose for a tiny sneeze and drifting seeds.
- **Cloud snow globe:** drag to shake it, then watch the snow settle inside.
- **Paper-boat pond:** guide the boat with a drag while its tray stays put.
- **Keepsake box:** open and close a soft little box containing mementos from these interactions. No streaks or locked features.
- **Dream bubbles:** occasional little dreams during quiet naps. Tap one for a heart without waking him. Study, reduced motion, and low battery suppress them.

The new props have soft illustrated artwork. Existing reaction strips accompany the globe, dandelion, boat, and keepsake interactions.

## Study your way

Under **Study together → Stopwatch & saved session**:

- Start an **open-ended study stopwatch**, with elapsed time instead of a countdown. Breaks still count down.
- **Resume your study bookmark** after restarting. Progress saves approximately every 15 seconds and on normal exit, alongside your existing goal. Restored sessions and sessions interrupted by laptop sleep wait paused until you resume.

## Desktop comfort

Open **Desktop comfort** in his pocket:

- **Keep-clear zones:** drag a rectangle into place, scroll to resize, then right-click to save. Escape cancels. Up to eight saved zones remain invisible; clear them from the same pocket. Automatic walks detour around them, chases stop at them, and surprise props avoid them. You can still drag him manually.
- **Undo the last move:** restore the last pet or bed drag, including both when they moved together.
- **Remembered monitor nooks:** keep geometry-based cozy-corner memories, with a safe fallback when a display disconnects. A pet settled in his corner can return with it when the preferred display reconnects.

Settings navigation stays unchanged. New activities live in the pocket, and opted-in surprise items can include the brush, dandelion, and globe.

## Install or update

Existing installations can use **App settings → Check for updates**, or their enabled automatic update preferences. The one-line installers also install the latest stable version.

Downloads:

- Apple Silicon Mac: `Cinnamoroll-0.2.0-aarch64-apple-darwin.zip`
- Intel Mac: `Cinnamoroll-0.2.0-x86_64-apple-darwin.zip`
- Windows x64: `Cinnamoroll-0.2.0-x86_64-pc-windows.zip`
- Source: `Cinnamoroll-0.2.0-source.zip`

macOS 13+ and Windows 10/11 x64 remain supported. Windows includes SDL and its required runtime; no separate Visual C++ runtime installation is needed. Signed update archives use the existing trusted update key.

## Notes

No new dependencies, accounts, background services, or screen inspection were added. Walking detours are cached, and effects remain bounded. Preferences and keepsakes stay local.

Multi-monitor layout and scaling still benefit from hands-on testing on each computer. The Mac app is ad-hoc signed and not notarized; the Windows executable is unsigned. Update archive signatures are separate from OS distribution signing.
