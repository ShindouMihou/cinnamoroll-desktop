# Cinnamoroll v0.1.2 · A little company ☁️

Our first cozy checkpoint: a small native desktop companion for study days, design nights, and little breaks together.

## What’s inside

- Smooth dedicated animation strips for affection, snacks, naps, music, dancing, hanging, walking, and more.
- Pick-up-and-carry dragging, gentle touch reactions, and a cute little pocket for direct interactions.
- A plush cloud bed, blankets, accessories, seasonal moments, and a quiet walk home after two minutes away without interaction.
- A study buddy with focus/break timers, subject presets, one local task, and quiet reading time.
- Treats, balls, bubbles, shuffled cups, ribbons, a wand, and a paper plane.
- One-line Mac and Windows installers with checksum verification, safe replacement, and per-user shortcuts.
- Clean native settings, reduced motion, launch at login, and local preferences.
- A signed GitHub update channel with optional updates and relaunching after an hour away.

## Downloads

Choose the ZIP for your computer and extract it completely:

- **Apple Silicon Mac:** `Cinnamoroll-0.1.2-aarch64-apple-darwin.zip`
- **Intel Mac:** `Cinnamoroll-0.1.2-x86_64-apple-darwin.zip`
- **Windows x64:** `Cinnamoroll-0.1.2-x86_64-pc-windows.zip`
- **Source:** `Cinnamoroll-0.1.2-source.zip`

Mac requires macOS 13 or later. Windows supports Windows 10 / 11 on Intel/AMD x64. Keep the app in a permanent writable location so future updates can replace it. On Windows, keep `SDL3.dll` beside `Cinnamoroll.exe`.

Right-click Cinnamoroll for the little pocket, or focus him and press **S** for settings. **App settings** contains launch at login and update options. Automatic checks and updates after an hour away start on; both can be switched off. Study sessions defer unattended installation. This first release bootstraps the signed channel for future versions.

## Checkpoint notes

The Mac app has an ad-hoc code signature and is not notarized; the Windows executable is unsigned. Archive signatures verify update authenticity and do not replace OS distribution signing. Platform-specific release packages must pass their native build, tests, launch checks, signature verification, and extracted-app version check before this draft is ready to publish.

Real dragging across displays, music-player coverage, and the full idle-update/relaunch experience still benefit from hands-on testing on each computer. The menu-bar item may be hidden on some Mac configurations; **S** remains available to open controls.
